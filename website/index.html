Why SpeakType

stays yours.

Most voice tools ship your audio to a cloud and rent it back to you. SpeakType keeps every byte on your Wi-Fi and every keystroke on your machine.

How it works

Three steps. Ten seconds.

  1. 1

    Pair

    Open Pair phone on the PC and scan the QR with the app. The code lives for 180 seconds; the pairing lives for good.

    QR → pinned TLS
  2. 2

    Speak

    Hold or tap on the phone. Its speech service recognizes your words on-device while the PC dashboard shows a live preview.

    speech → text
  3. 3

    Typed

    The PC formats and types the final text into whatever window you focused. Click a field, talk, done.

    final → keystrokes

Router gave your PC a new IP? Reconnect rescans the subnet and re-validates the pinned certificate — no fresh QR scan needed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

No. Audio never leaves the phone — recognition runs on the phone itself. Only text travels to your PC over pinned TLS on your Wi-Fi. No cloud relay, no account, no telemetry.

The phone uses its built-in speech recognition service — Google on most phones, with optional offline language packs. Any Android 8.0+ phone with a recognition service works. The PC needs no speech software and no model downloads.

Bangla in Bengali script, Banglish (phonetic Latin spellings), and English. You pick the output format on the phone; the PC applies it to every transcript.

Tap Reconnect. The app rescans your subnet and re-validates the pinned certificate, so a router-assigned IP change doesn’t force a new QR scan. A changed certificate does — that’s the pinning doing its job.

Click the target text field first. SpeakType refuses to type on secure desktops (sign-in, UAC), and elevated apps can block input from a non-elevated app. A small typing delay in Settings helps apps that drop fast input.

The PC serves the newest complete APK it can find. The phone compares SHA-256 hashes and offers the update only when the files differ; you confirm the system install dialog.
